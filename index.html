Which service fits your water problem

If you need water damage restoration in La Puente, CA, the first question is what the water is doing right now. Standing water needs pumps and extraction, a wet wall needs drying equipment, and a damp room that smells musty may already need mold work. We dispatch from La Puente, and the six services below cover those situations in the order most homeowners meet them.

Emergency Water Removal

Pumps and extraction units pull standing water out of floors and carpet after a burst pipe, a failed water heater or an overflowing tub.

Water Mitigation

Moisture meters find hidden wet spots, then air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure so the damage stops spreading in the first days.

Water Damage Cleanup

Wet debris and soaked materials come out, surfaces are cleaned and sanitized, and the room is readied for rebuilding.

Flood Damage Restoration

Storm runoff, groundwater and sewage backup bring contaminated water, which calls for stricter removal and disposal than a clean supply line leak.

Water Damage Repair

Once everything reads dry, drywall, insulation, paint and flooring are replaced so the room looks and works like it did before.

Mold Remediation

When moisture has sat long enough for mold to grow, crews contain the area, filter the air and remove affected material after the leak is fixed.

Not sure which one you need? Describe the room, the water source and how long it has been wet when you call, and dispatch will point you to the right starting service. Most real jobs use two or three of them in sequence.

The first hour

What to do in the first hour while you wait

Homeowners in La Puente usually call us from a kitchen, a hallway or a garage floor with water spreading toward carpet. What you do before the crew arrives matters, because wet drywall and subfloor begin to degrade quickly. These four steps cost nothing and protect your home and your insurance claim.

Stop the source

Turn off the small valve under the fixture that is leaking, or the main valve if you cannot tell where it is coming from. The main is typically by the meter or on the street side of the house. If the water is from rain or a backed-up drain, skip ahead to the next step.

Cut power only if it is safe

Circuits serving wet rooms can be switched off at the breaker box, but only when you can get to it over dry floor. Water around outlets or the panel is a job for your utility or 911, so stay away from it.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, electronics, books and small furniture to dry ground. Put foil or plastic under furniture legs on wet carpet so wood finishes do not stain.

Photograph, then call

Take wide and close photos of the water line and damaged items, then call (626) 879-0276. Sewer or outdoor water is not safe for kids or animals to wade through, so move them to a dry room.

The EPA's advice is a drying clock of one to two days, since mold gets a foothold on wet drywall and wood after that. That window is why we ask you to call before cleaning up on your own. Our emergency water removal crews start with extraction, and water mitigation picks up from there.

Local knowledge

What La Puente homes mean for drying and claims

The city sits on roughly 3.48 square miles and calls itself mostly residential. That means most calls come from houses, duplexes and apartments packed close together, so a leak in one unit can reach a neighbor's wall quickly. Tell dispatch if the water is crossing a shared wall or ceiling, because both sides may need drying.

The city reports about six in ten homes are owner-occupied, so many callers are tenants as well as owners. If you rent, tell your landlord right away and keep your own photos, since the owner's policy and a renter's policy cover different things. Ask your insurer which one applies.

Water service also varies by address. The La Puente Valley County Water District serves part of the city, and Suburban Water Systems lists La Puente among its areas, so check your water bill to see who to call about the meter or a street-side shutoff.

Beneath the streets, the Maintenance Division reports 63.2 miles of city sewer line and 1,386 manholes. If a backup seems to start outside your home, ask the city who handles the line before you assume the blockage is yours. Our flood damage restoration crews handle the cleanup either way.

Rebuild work that triggers a permit goes through the city's Building and Safety Division for plan review and inspection. A contractor who pulls permits here must hold a city business license and a state contractor card, so it is reasonable to ask to see both.

Be ready

Insurance documentation checklist

Good records make a claim conversation simpler, whatever your insurer decides. Gather these while the crew is working, and keep every receipt.

Phone photos with timestamps are usually enough, so you do not need special equipment. Take them before anything is moved, then again after the crew has pulled out wet material, so the full extent of the loss is visible later.

  • Your policy number and your insurer's claim phone number
  • Wide and close photos of every wet room, the water line on walls and damaged belongings
  • A list of damaged items with approximate age and where you bought them
  • The date and time you noticed the water and what you did to stop it
  • Names and phone numbers of anyone you spoke with at your insurer
  • Receipts for emergency purchases such as plastic sheeting or hotel stays
  • Daily moisture readings and the drying log kept by the crew

Keep every receipt, do only what limits more damage, and leave permanent fixes until your adjuster has seen the room. Dried-out rooms can wait a few days for rebuilding, so ask your insurer before you approve any water damage repair work.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you get to my home in La Puente?

Dispatch answers 24/7 and offers same-day response when a crew is available. We do not promise an exact arrival time, because traffic and active jobs change it. The dispatcher will tell you what is realistic when you call.

Do I need to call my insurance company first?

Call us first if water is still spreading, since stopping the damage comes before paperwork. Then contact your insurer as soon as you can and ask what your policy requires. Coverage differs between policies, so check your policy.

What is the difference between water mitigation and water damage repair?

Mitigation is the emergency work that removes water and dries the structure. Repair is the rebuilding afterward, such as new drywall, paint and flooring. Repair only makes sense once moisture readings show the materials are dry.

Is flood damage covered by homeowners insurance?

Generally no. Flood cover is bought apart from a standard homeowners policy, either through the federal NFIP or from a private insurer. Ask your insurer what your own policy says.

How long does it take to dry a house after water damage?

Drying commonly takes several days, depending on the water category, the materials and how much area is wet. At each visit the crew re-reads the meter, and the fans and dehumidifiers stay until wood and drywall test dry. Your crew can give a better estimate after inspecting.

Do you work on mold as well as water damage?

Yes, mold remediation is available when moisture has already caused growth. The leak has to be fixed first, and work follows ANSI/IICRC S520 practice. Any health questions belong with your physician.
