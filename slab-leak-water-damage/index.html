How slab leaks happen in La Puente homes

Many homes here are built on slab foundations, which means the pipes that carry water to the kitchen, baths and laundry often run through or beneath poured concrete. Over decades, a line can corrode, rub against rock or concrete, or fail at a joint. The water has nowhere to go but up into the flooring or sideways under the walls.

The city says La Puente is primarily residential, and Census QuickFacts show owners living in 60.4 percent of homes. If you own, a hidden leak is your repair and your records to keep. If you rent, tell your landlord the day you notice a symptom, in writing.

On a slab there is no crawl space to peek into, so the first sign is usually on the floor or on the meter. That is why a small clue deserves a quick check instead of a wait-and-see week.

First-hour safety steps when water is spreading

Before you touch the wet floor

A leak with pressure behind it can wet outlets, appliances and the base of walls in minutes.

  • Shut off the main water valve, usually at the meter box or on the house at the front or side.
  • Gas smell, hissing or sparks means leave and call 911 or the gas utility before calling anyone else.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms only from a dry panel while standing on dry ground, otherwise stay out.
  • Keep children and pets off warm or soaked floors, and do not use a vacuum on standing water.
  • Call a licensed plumber or leak detection company, then call us at (626) 879-0276.

Our drying process for slab leak damage

Map the moisture

A moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera show how far the water ran, including into walls and under cabinet bases.

Open and lift

Floating floors and wet baseboards come up, and carpet is lifted so air can reach the pad and subfloor.

Dry with airflow

Air movers push air across the surface while LGR dehumidifiers pull water out of the room air.

Track it daily

Readings are logged each visit until the materials reach dry standards, using ANSI/IICRC S500 as the reference.

Concrete holds water and gives it up slowly, so a slab can keep feeding moisture into a floor long after the pipe is fixed. That is why the pipe repair and the drying need to be coordinated, and why we check readings again before anything is rebuilt.

We are not plumbers, so we do not find or repair the leak, and we do not do foundation repair or waterproofing. The plumber or leak detection company decides whether to spot repair the line, reroute it through the walls or attic, or replace it. Once it is dry, water damage repair covers the rebuild, and early extraction is described on the emergency water removal page.

Insurance and claim questions for a slab leak

The California Department of Insurance says homeowners policies cover many kinds of water damage but not flood, and that policies may not cover ensuing damage from a problem left alone. A sudden, accidental break is often treated differently from a leak that dripped for months, so read your policy before you assume either way.

The department also advises against ignoring an obvious water problem. Photograph the wet areas, keep receipts, and ask your insurer whether the cost to reach the pipe under the slab is part of a claim, because that wording varies. We cannot promise a claim outcome.

Mistakes to avoid after a slab leak

  • Waiting for the bill to settle the question, since water keeps soaking in meanwhile
  • Laying new flooring over a floor that has not been checked with a meter
  • Running a heater to dry the floor, which can push moisture into the walls
  • Skipping the call to your water provider about the meter or the bill

Yes. Our 24/7 dispatch line takes calls any hour, and same-day response is available when a crew is free. Shut the main valve first and have the area ready to describe.

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in La Puente?

Yes, nights and weekends are handled like any other call. We do not promise a set arrival time, but the dispatcher will give you safe steps while a crew is lined up.

Can you find the slab leak?

No. Locating and repairing a supply line is the work of a plumber or leak detection company. We measure how far the water traveled and dry the flooring, subfloor and walls.

Do you need to remove my floors?

It depends on the material and what the meter reads. Floating floors and baseboards are often lifted, while some solid surfaces can be dried in place.

How long does drying take after a slab leak?

Several days is common, but the meter readings decide, not the calendar. Concrete can release moisture slowly, so readings are rechecked before the rebuild.

Does homeowners insurance cover a slab leak?

Sometimes, depending on the policy and whether the break was sudden or gradual. Check your policy and ask your insurer, because we cannot promise any outcome.
