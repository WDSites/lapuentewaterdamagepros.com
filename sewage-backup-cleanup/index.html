Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in La Puente, CA

When a toilet, tub or floor drain pushes waste into the house, the job is containment first and cleaning second.

Sewage backup cleanup in La Puente begins the moment waste water rises through a toilet, tub or floor drain, and our 24/7 dispatch line at (626) 879-0276 is ready for it. Stop using every drain in the house, including the washer and dishwasher, since each flush or load sends more water to the same blockage.

Keep people and pets out of the affected rooms, and do not try to mop it up. We contain the area, remove the water and the materials it soaked, then clean and sanitize.

Sewage backup emergency: first safety steps

Backup water is Category 3 in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which means it is grossly contaminated and may carry bacteria and other organisms. Treat every wet surface as contaminated, even if the water looks mostly clear. Close doors to the affected rooms, and keep hands, bare feet and children away from the water.

The EPA advises that sewage or contaminated water warrants a professional. That matches what we see, since household cleaners do not reach into wet drywall and carpet pad, and a wet vacuum can spray droplets into the air.

Sewage safety before anyone steps in

Contaminated water and electricity are a bad mix, so slow down at the door.

  • Do not walk through the water, and do not use a household vacuum on it.
  • Shut off power to wet rooms only from a dry panel while standing on dry ground.
  • Gas smell, hissing or sparks means leave and call 911 or the gas utility first.
  • Stop running water in the house, because every drain feeds the same blocked line.
  • Wash hands with soap after any contact and keep food and dishes out of the area.

Where backups come from and who owns the blockage

A backup comes from either the city's main under the street or the home's own lateral, the pipe that runs from your house to the main. The City of La Puente Maintenance Division says it is responsible for 63.2 miles of city sewers and 1,386 street manholes. For you, that is a useful clue because a blocked main often affects several nearby homes, while a blocked lateral usually affects only yours.

Ask a neighbor if they see the same problem, and call the city Maintenance Division if you think the main is involved. If the blockage is on your side, a licensed plumber clears it, and often runs a camera to see roots or a broken section. We do not clear or repair sewer lines.

Our process: containment, removal and sanitizing

Contain

Crews wear protective gear and may hang plastic sheeting and run negative air containment so air is pulled toward the work zone.

Extract

Truck-mounted and portable extraction units remove standing water before anything is carried out.

Remove porous materials

Carpet, pad, wet drywall and insulation that soaked up Category 3 water are cut out, bagged and removed.

Clean and sanitize

Surfaces are washed with detergent, treated with an antimicrobial for its contact time, then dried with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers.

A moisture meter shows where wet material stops, and a thermal imaging camera helps spot water hiding behind walls. HEPA vacuums and HEPA air scrubbers handle fine dust from tear out, and the odor goes away mostly because the contaminated materials are gone.

We are not plumbers, so we do not clear the line. We are also not electricians, and we do not do foundation repair or waterproofing. Related pages explain the next steps, including water damage cleanup for debris and sanitizing and flood damage restoration when storm water is the source.

Insurance and claim questions after a sewage backup

A sudden backup is often handled differently from a flood, and many policies treat sewer or drain backup as an optional add-on or a limited item. The California Department of Insurance says homeowners policies do not cover flood but do cover other kinds of water damage. Read your policy, look for the word backup, and ask your insurer what it covers. We cannot promise a claim outcome.

The department also advises keeping receipts, photographing damage and not discarding damaged items until the adjuster approves. Take photos before cleanup, and ask whether the adjuster wants to see the materials first.

Mistakes to avoid after a sewage backup

  • Flushing or running the washer to see whether the line has cleared
  • Mixing bleach with other cleaners
  • Keeping wet carpet and pad because it looks clean
  • Letting children or pets walk through the room
  • Starting repairs before the plumber has found the cause

Ask the city Building & Safety division about permits if plumbing or electrical pieces must be replaced after demolition. We dispatch from La Puente to West Covina and Baldwin Park as well.

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency sewage backup cleanup available in La Puente?

Yes. Our 24/7 dispatch line takes calls any hour, and same-day response is available when a crew is free. Stop using drains and keep everyone out of the area while you wait.

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in La Puente?

Yes. Backups do not keep office hours, so night and weekend calls are handled like any other. No arrival time is promised, but the dispatcher gives safe steps for the wait.

Is the city or the homeowner responsible for the blockage?

It depends on where the blockage sits. The city maintains the sewers in the street, and the pipe from your home to the main is usually the owner's. Ask the city Maintenance Division and a plumber to confirm.

Can I clean up sewage water myself?

The EPA advises a professional for sewage or contaminated water. Porous materials hold contamination that household cleaners do not reach.

Will the smell go away?

Usually yes, once contaminated materials are removed and surfaces are cleaned, treated and dried. Odor that lingers often points to wet material that was missed.

Do you unclog the drain or repair the sewer line?

No. A licensed plumber clears and repairs the line. We handle the water and the damage it caused.
