Before you call

Protect people first.

  • Do not touch switches or breakers while standing in water. Switch off power only if the panel is dry and reachable.
  • Smell gas or see sparks? Leave and call 911 or the gas utility first, then call the dispatch line.
  • Keep children and pets away from sewage or murky water, and do not wade in it.
  • Photograph the damage from a dry spot before you move anything.

How extraction and drying work

Standing water comes out first with truck-mounted or portable extraction units. Moisture meters then show how far water has traveled into drywall, framing and flooring, and thermal imaging cameras find wet spots behind surfaces. EPA guidance sets the target of drying within 24 to 48 hours. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published industry standard, so ask any company whether its crews work to it.

Air movers push dry air across surfaces while LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the room. Not everything can be saved, and soaked pad, swollen particleboard or contaminated materials may be removed.

We do not do plumbing, roofing or electrical repair. A plumber fixes the source, and we handle the water that source left behind.

Documenting a loss for your insurer

The California Department of Insurance advises homeowners to document damage with photos and keep receipts. Coverage depends on the cause and your policy terms, so check your policy and ask your insurer.

  • Dated photos and video of the damage and affected belongings
  • Moisture readings and a written, itemized scope of work
  • Your policy number and the date and cause of the loss
