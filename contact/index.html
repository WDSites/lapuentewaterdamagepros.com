Contact La Puente Water Damage Pros

One number, answered around the clock, and no forms to fill out.

The way to reach La Puente Water Damage Pros is by phone at (626) 879-0276. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, because pipes burst and roofs leak on their own schedule.

We do not use contact forms, chat boxes or booking widgets. A person on the line can ask the follow-up questions a form would miss, and that gets a crew moving faster.

Call the dispatch line

Dial (626) 879-0276 from any phone. If the water is active, say so in your first sentence so we can prioritize your call. Same-day visits happen when a crew is free. What we will not do is invent an arrival time, so you get a plain description of what to expect.

Call-only service

Phone is the only way to request help. Please do not look for a form on this site, because there is not one.

What to say on the call

A few clear details help the dispatcher send the right crew with the right equipment. You do not need technical language. Describe what you see, in your own words.

Give the address

State the street address and city, and mention a gate code or a hard-to-find entrance if there is one.

Describe the water

Say where it is coming from, if you know, and whether it is clean supply water, rain, or something that smells like a drain or sewer.

Tell us what is wet

Name the rooms and surfaces, such as carpet, hardwood, drywall or a ceiling that is sagging or dripping.

Mention what you have done

Let us know if you shut off the main water valve, switched off power to wet areas, or moved furniture and valuables.

What to have ready

You can call before any of this is gathered, since safety comes first. When you have a spare minute, though, the following will speed up the job and help any insurance claim you decide to make.

  • Photos and a short video of the damage taken before anything is moved, including the water source and any visible stains
  • Your homeowners policy number and the phone number of your insurer, which is usually on your declarations page or insurance card
  • A note of when you first noticed the water and what you think caused it
  • The location of your main water shutoff and your electrical panel, so the crew can find them quickly
  • Receipts for any emergency supplies you buy, such as plastic sheeting or a wet vacuum rental

We cannot tell you whether your policy will pay, and we do not bill insurers directly. Your insurer or agent is the right place to ask about coverage.

Before you dial: safety first

If you see any of these, act before calling us

Your safety matters more than the water.

  • If you smell gas or see sparks, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility
  • If water is touching outlets, appliances or a breaker panel, stay out and do not touch electrical equipment
  • If the water might be sewage, keep people and pets out of it
  • If anyone is hurt or trapped, call 911 first

Frequently asked questions

Is the phone line really open at night?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7, and overnight callers get the same questions and the same crew matching as daytime callers.

Can I send photos instead of calling?

Start with a call. The dispatcher will explain how photos can be shared with the crew assigned to your job once you are connected.

Will you tell me the price on the phone?

We can explain what drives cost, such as water category, square footage, materials and drying days. The assigned crew explains scope and price before work starts, after seeing the damage.
