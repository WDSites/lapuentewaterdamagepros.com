How dispatch covers nearby towns

Everyone who calls (626) 879-0276 reaches the same 24/7 dispatch line, whichever town they live in. The dispatcher confirms your address and the source of the water, then assigns a local licensed and insured crew with the right equipment.

The three neighboring towns sit a short drive from our base, which is why a crew can frequently be sent that same day. We never promise an exact arrival time, because active jobs and traffic on the freeways around the valley change it hour to hour.

How a service area works

A service area is simply the set of addresses a dispatch line can send crews to on the same day. It is not a franchise boundary or a city limit, and a ZIP code does not always follow a town line. For that reason, we list ZIP codes per town and also ask for your full street address on the call.

Once dispatch has your address, they match the job to the nearest available crew. A sewage backup in West Covina and a kitchen leak in Baldwin Park may leave from the same base, but they carry different equipment.

La Puente

La Puente is our home base, covering ZIP codes 91744 and 91746. At roughly 3.48 square miles and mostly homes, La Puente packs houses close together, and a leak can travel through a shared wall. Start with emergency water removal if water is standing, or water mitigation if walls are damp.

The city reports about 60 percent of homes are owner-occupied, so renters call us often too. Tell your landlord quickly, because the building owner's insurance and your renter's policy cover different things.

Baldwin Park

Baldwin Park lies northwest of La Puente and uses ZIP code 91706. Baldwin Park reports that most buildings predate 1960, so expect aging plumbing and materials that deserve a careful moisture scan. Details are on our Baldwin Park page, and water damage cleanup is a common request there.

Baldwin Park's own website says FEMA has drawn neither 100-year nor 500-year flood zones inside city limits. An unmapped city still has rain and broken mains, so look your address up on FEMA's map and ask your insurer what is and is not included.

West Covina

West Covina lies directly east of La Puente and is served under ZIP codes 91790, 91791 and 91792. According to the draft housing element, over two thirds of West Covina homes date from before 1980, which points to older supply lines and water heaters. Read the West Covina page or the guide to water damage repair if the leak is already dry.

Most West Covina housing is single-family detached, so a leak is often contained to one house. Even so, a slab leak or a roof leak can run for days inside walls before it shows, and moisture meters find what the eye cannot.

Hacienda Heights

Hacienda Heights lies south of La Puente along Hacienda Blvd and uses ZIP code 91745. It is an unincorporated community governed by Los Angeles County, so ask the county about permit rules. See the Hacienda Heights page and our flood damage restoration service for storm-related water.

Hacienda Heights has steep slopes in places, per the county plan, and a hillside slide near Gotera Drive in February 2024 showed what hard rain can do to soil. If runoff enters a lower level or garage, call right away and keep clear of electrical risk.

Before you call

  • Say where the water came from and which rooms are wet
  • Mention any sewage, storm water or mold you can see
  • Keep your policy number nearby for later
  • Take photos before moving anything
