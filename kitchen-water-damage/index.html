Kitchen Water Damage in La Puente, CA

Water under the sink spreads into cabinets, toe kicks and floors before you notice it.

Kitchen water damage often begins under the sink. A supply line weeps, a drain fitting loosens, or a dishwasher connection drips into a cabinet box that nobody opens for weeks. The particleboard swells, the floor edge lifts, and the cabinet base holds moisture long after the puddle is gone.

Crews dispatch from La Puente, with 24/7 dispatch and same-day response when available. Here is how kitchen leaks are found, dried and repaired, and when cabinets have to come out.

How kitchen water damage starts in La Puente homes

Census QuickFacts put the median value of owner-occupied homes in La Puente at $617,700, and owners hold about 60.4 percent of housing. Kitchens carry a big share of that value in cabinets, counters and flooring, so a leak that quietly ruins a cabinet run can be expensive to ignore. Open the sink cabinet and look at the floor of it once in a while.

The usual culprits are flexible supply lines, the compression nuts under the faucet, a loose drain trap, and the water line to a dishwasher or filter. Garbage disposals add another seam to leak at. Water follows gravity into the cabinet floor, then out under the toe kick to the subfloor.

Signs the leak has reached the cabinets and floor

Swollen cabinet floor

Particleboard expands when wet and rarely shrinks back. A soft or crumbling edge inside the sink base usually means the box is finished.

Lifting toe kick or baseboard

Edges curl or separate from the wall when moisture climbs from the floor. Check where cabinets meet the wall.

Cupped or gapped flooring

Laminate seams rise, vinyl planks buckle and hardwood edges crown near the sink and dishwasher.

Musty smell and dark staining

A smell under the sink with no visible puddle points to a slow drip. Staining on the back panel often shows how long it has run.

Moisture meters read the cabinet sides, floor edge and wall base to tell wet from dry. A thermal imaging camera helps find water that has moved behind the cabinets. We then decide what can be dried in place.

Kitchen leak safety and the first hour

Before you start wiping

Kitchens combine water with outlets, appliances and sometimes gas, so slow down.

  • Turn off the angle stop under the sink, or the main water valve if the stop will not close.
  • Leave the dishwasher and disposal off. Switch off that circuit only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • If you smell gas, see sparks or find water near a range outlet, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility first.
  • Empty the cabinet, set things on a dry surface and keep children and pets away from the wet floor.
  • Call a plumber for the failed line and (626) 879-0276 for the damage.

How we dry kitchens and when cabinets come out

Extraction comes first, using portable units to pull water from the floor. Toe kicks are removed so air can reach the space behind the cabinets, and small access holes may be cut in the wall base. Air movers push dry air through cabinet cavities while LGR dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the room.

A cabinet is saved when the box is solid wood or sealed material and the meter reaches dry values within a few days. It is pulled when particleboard has swelled, when the box sat in water, or when water got in the wall behind it. Pulling a base cabinet is a bigger step, so you get the reasoning and the moisture readings before it happens.

Insurance and kitchen leak claims

The California Department of Insurance advises photographing damage, keeping receipts for reasonable protection costs and holding off on extensive permanent repairs until the adjuster has inspected. That matters in a kitchen, where the instinct is to tear out cabinets right away. Take dated photos first, and ask your insurer what it wants to see.

Sudden and accidental leaks are often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage is usually excluded and needs a separate flood policy. Check your policy. We cannot promise any claim outcome or amount.

Mistakes to avoid and how to prevent kitchen leaks

  • Do not store cleaners and paper goods flat on a damp cabinet floor.
  • Do not caulk or paint over swollen board. It hides moisture without drying it.
  • Do not wait for it to dry on its own. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold.
  • Check supply lines for rust, bulges and drips when you change a faucet.
  • Run a hand along the back of the sink base and the dishwasher side for dampness every few months.

Washers and refrigerators have their own page on appliance leak water damage. If a musty smell keeps returning after drying, ask about mold remediation.

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency kitchen water damage service available in La Puente?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7 and same-day response is available when crews are free. No arrival time is promised, but you get safe steps while you wait.

Can wet kitchen cabinets be saved?

Solid or sealed boxes often can if they are dried quickly. Swollen particleboard usually cannot, because it expands and does not return to shape.

Do you remove the toe kick during drying?

Often yes. It gives airflow to the hidden space behind the cabinet, and it is simple to reinstall.

What if the leak was from the dishwasher?

Dishwasher discharge can carry soil, so it may be treated as Category 2 water. We check the floor and cabinet side, and a plumber should fix the connection.

How do I know if my kitchen floor is still wet?

Dry to the touch is not proof. A moisture meter on the floor edge and cabinet base gives a real reading.

Does homeowners insurance cover a kitchen leak?

It may when the damage is sudden and accidental, but gradual leaks are often excluded. Ask your insurer and check your policy.

Can someone come look before I decide on tear-out?

Yes. We can schedule an assessment and explain the readings and what should be removed before anything is cut.
